# Rain Probability & Precipitation Guidance

## What Does Probability of Precipitation (PoP) Mean?
Probability of Precipitation (PoP) describes the chance of receiving measurable rainfall (at least 0.1 mm or 0.01 inches) at a specific point in the forecast area during a given time period.

Mathematically, PoP is defined as:
PoP = C x A
where C is the confidence that rain will occur somewhere in the forecast area, and A is the percent of the area that will receive measurable rain.

## Common Misconceptions
- **Misconception 1:** A 70% chance of rain does NOT mean it will rain for 70% of the day.
- **Misconception 2:** A 70% chance of rain does NOT mean 70% of the city will be covered in rain at all times.
- **Fact:** It means there is a 70% statistical likelihood that rain will fall on your specific location during the forecast window.

## Rain Intensity Classifications
- **Light Rain:** Less than 2.5 mm per hour. Minimal disruption to outdoor activities.
- **Moderate Rain:** 2.5 mm to 7.5 mm per hour. Carrying an umbrella or raincoat is recommended.
- **Heavy Rain:** 7.5 mm to 50 mm per hour. Urban flooding potential; reduce driving speeds.
- **Violent Rain / Torrential:** Greater than 50 mm per hour. Flash flood warnings; stay indoors.

## Rain Preparedness Guidance
- **PoP < 30%:** Low likelihood of rain. Outdoor plans can generally proceed without rain gear.
- **PoP 30% - 60%:** Moderate likelihood. Carrying a compact umbrella or light raincoat is recommended.
- **PoP > 60%:** High likelihood of rain. Keep rain protection handy, secure outdoor belongings, and monitor hourly forecasts.
