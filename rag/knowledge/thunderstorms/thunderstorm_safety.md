# Thunderstorm Safety & Severe Weather Guidance

## What Causes Thunderstorms?
Thunderstorms develop when warm, moist air rises rapidly into cooler atmospheric layers, creating strong updrafts and downdrafts. Lightning, heavy rain, strong winds, and hail are common hazards.

## Safety Rules During Thunderstorms

### Immediate Action (30/30 Rule)
- If you hear thunder, you are close enough to be struck by lightning.
- **Rule:** Seek shelter immediately when thunder sounds. Stay indoors until 30 minutes after the last clap of thunder.

### Indoor Safety Precautions
- Stay away from windows, doors, and electrical appliances.
- Do not use corded phones or touch plumbing pipes (lightning can travel through metal pipes and wiring).
- Unplug sensitive electronic equipment to prevent damage from power surges.

### Outdoor Safety Precautions (If Caught Outside)
- Never shelter under isolated trees, metal towers, or tall objects.
- Get out of open water bodies (lakes, rivers, pools).
- Seek shelter inside a hard-topped metal vehicle with windows rolled up.
- If no shelter is available, crouch low on the balls of your feet with your head tucked and ears covered. Minimize contact with the ground.
