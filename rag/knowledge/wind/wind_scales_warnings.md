# Wind Speeds, Beaufort Scale & Safety Precautions

## Wind Speed Classifications (Beaufort Scale Excerpt)

### Light to Moderate Breeze (1 - 28 km/h)
- Leaves rustle, small twigs move, flags extend.
- Safe for all normal outdoor activities.

### Fresh to Strong Breeze (29 - 50 km/h)
- Large branches move; umbrellas used with difficulty; telephone wires whistle.
- Take care with lightweight outdoor furniture and open doors carefully.

### High Wind / Gale (51 - 75 km/h)
- Whole trees in motion; inconvenience felt when walking against wind; twigs break off trees.
- Secure loose outdoor objects, avoid high-profile vehicles on open bridges.

### Severe Gale / Storm (76 - 117 km/h)
- Slight to structural damage (chimney pots and slates removed, trees uprooted).
- Remain indoors away from windows. Avoid driving.
