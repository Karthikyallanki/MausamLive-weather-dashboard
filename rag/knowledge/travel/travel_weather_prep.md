# Travel Weather Preparation Guidance

## Pre-Trip Weather Checklist
When traveling to new destinations or microclimates:
1. **Check 7-Day & Hourly Forecasts:** Monitor temperature swings, rain probability, and severe weather warnings 48 hours prior to departure.
2. **Pack Microclimate Apparel:** Layered clothing adapts well to daytime heat and nighttime temperature drops.
3. **Rain & Storm Contingency:** Always carry a compact umbrella, waterproof footwear, and waterproof device sleeves if destination rain probability exceeds 50%.
4. **UV & Altitude Factor:** High altitude destinations double UV exposure. Bring high SPF sunscreen and UV sunglasses even in cold alpine weather.
5. **Transit Delays:** Allow extra commuting buffer time during fog, heavy downpours, or high wind alerts.
